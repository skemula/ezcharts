SETUP

From desk to markets in four steps

No account walls, no config screens. EZ Charts is designed to get your market display running quickly, without complicated setup or unnecessary menus.

01

Unbox & power on

Set it on your desk and plug it in. The screen wakes straight into setup.

02

Connect it to wifi

The device connects to your WiFi during setup. If it can't find a network, it creates its own setup connection so you can quickly pair it from your phone.

03

Configure your displays

Open the EZ Charts desktop app, choose the assets you follow, and create your saved market views for the device.

04

Press the button

Press the button on the device to cycle through your saved market views without opening another app or browser tab.

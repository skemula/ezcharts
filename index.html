FAQ

Good to know

Does the device work without the desktop app?

The desktop app is how you choose which assets show up and build your five saved displays. Once configured, the device runs on its own — you only need the app again to make changes.

What happens if my Wi-Fi drops?

The device reconnects automatically once your network is back and keeps showing your last saved display in the meantime.

Does it need my computer running?

No. Once configured, EZ Charts runs independently and reconnects automatically.

When can I buy one?

EZ Charts isn't for sale yet. Hardware is currently in prototyping.

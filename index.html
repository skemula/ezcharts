Why we build this way

A screen for one job, not forty tabs.

General-purpose screens are full of things fighting for your attention. Xylnt devices are built to show one thing clearly, stay out of your way, and keep working after you've stopped thinking about them.

Nothing else on it

No notifications, no tabs, no login walls. Just the one thing you set it to show.

No app required

Everything is set up right on the device itself — connect it to Wi‑Fi and you're done.

Keeps working

Drops off Wi‑Fi, reconnects on its own. It's meant to be plugged in once and forgotten.

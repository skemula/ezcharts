SETUP

From desk to markets in four steps

No account walls, no config screens. EZ Charts is designed to get your market display running quickly, without complicated setup or unnecessary menus.

01

Unbox & power on

Set it on your desk and plug it in. The screen wakes straight into setup.

02

Connect to Wi-Fi

The device connects to your WiFi during setup. If it can't find a network, it creates its own setup connection so you can quickly pair it from your phone.

03

Configure your displays

Open the EZ Charts desktop app, choose the assets you follow, and create your saved market views for the device.

04

Press the button

Press the button on the device to cycle through your saved market views without opening another app or browser tab.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a subscription?

No. EZ Charts is a one-time purchase — the device and the desktop app are both included, with no recurring fees.

Does the device work without the desktop app?

The desktop app is how you choose which assets show up and build your five saved displays. Once configured, the device runs on its own — you only need the app again to make changes.

What happens if my Wi-Fi drops?

The device reconnects automatically once your network is back and keeps showing your last saved display in the meantime.

Which markets can I display?

Crypto, stocks, ETFs, indexes and commodities — anything covered in the Markets section above, with more added over time.

When can I buy one?

EZ Charts isn't for sale yet. Hardware is in prototyping now — check the roadmap above for where things stand.
